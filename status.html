⚠ Ongoing: domain delegation points at the wrong Cloudflare account

The registry nameservers for brainsait.org, elfadil.com and sibaiti.app currently point at a Cloudflare zone in a different account, so our own zone is parked as pending / ns_mismatch. Cloudflare permanently bans cross-account CNAMEs, which is why every worker-backed subdomain answers error 1014 and every tunnel answers 1033. Nothing here is a fault of the services themselves — the origins are healthy.

The fix is one change: set the nameservers to jewel.ns.cloudflare.com + milan.ns.cloudflare.com — then every surface below returns on its real domain with no code change.

A green dot means a real 2xx was observed. A blue dot means the endpoint answered but the browser was not allowed to read its status — reachability, not health.
